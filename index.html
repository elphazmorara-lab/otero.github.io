About

I prepare quantity takeoffs, bills of quantities and cost estimates from architectural and structural drawings, and I have worked on site as a clerk of works. That mix means my numbers are checked against what is actually being built.

I focus on controlling cost, reducing variations and keeping cost documents accurate, organised and easy to audit. I have worked within public procurement rules and contract documents, and I give clear progress and cost updates to contractors, consultants and project committees.

Services

  • Quantity takeoff

    Quantities measured from architectural, structural and services drawings: substructure, superstructure, finishes and external works.

  • Bills of quantities

    BQs and rate build-ups prepared in line with the Standard Method of Measurement.

  • Cost estimating

    Elemental estimates, tender pricing and FF&E estimating, updated as drawings and scope change.

  • Valuations

    Interim valuations, variations, final accounts and cost-to-complete reporting against the contract sum.

  • Site measurement

    Measuring work done, checking materials and workmanship against drawings and specifications, and keeping site records.
